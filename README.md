## 🧠 About Me

I'm driven by a simple question: **how do systems actually behave under pressure — at scale, under failure, under real users?**

My work lives at the intersection of **backend engineering**, **automation**, and **applied intelligence**. I don't chase frameworks for the sake of novelty — I use projects as a lab to explore system design, architectural trade-offs, and the messy constraints that only show up once something is actually running in production.

Right now, I'm going deep on **Python internals**, **backend architecture at scale**, and the **mathematical and engineering foundations of AI/ML** — not to use them as black boxes, but to actually understand what's happening underneath.

> **Philosophy:** Most software is built to *work*. I try to build software that's built to *last, scale, and think*.
